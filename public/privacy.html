Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

1. Overview & Local-First Architecture

Habits (“Habits — Habit & Task Tracker”) is a client-side, local-first web application. Your habits, check-ins, daily notes, and tasks are stored directly inside your browser’s IndexedDB storage and preferences in localStorage.

Habits does not operate an application backend server. The developer does not collect, store, sell, rent, or share your personal habit records, tasks, or Google account credentials.

2. Guest Mode vs. Google Sign-In Mode

  • Guest Mode: Operates completely offline in your browser and never sends requests to Google APIs or external servers.
  • Google Sign-In Mode (Optional): Uses Google Identity Services directly in your browser to sync encrypted/binary backups with your personal Google Drive and optionally schedule reminders in an isolated calendar.

3. Google User Data & Permissions Accessed

When you choose to connect your Google account, Habits requests only the minimum permissions required for these user-initiated features:

  • Google Drive App Data Folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): Used exclusively to store, merge, inspect version history of, and restore your personal habits-snapshot.bin backup file inside Google Drive’s hidden app-specific folder. Habits cannot view, read, or modify any other files on your Google Drive.
  • App-Created Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created): Used only when you enable Calendar reminders to create and manage a dedicated Habits Reminders secondary calendar and add/update/delete reminder events for pending habits and tasks. Habits cannot access or modify your personal Google Calendars.
  • Account Email Hint: Obtained from Google Drive’s account metadata solely to display which Google account is connected and pre-fill reconnection prompts inside your browser.

4. Token Handling & Security

OAuth access tokens granted by Google are kept exclusively in your browser’s active memory (RAM) for the duration of the session and expire automatically. Access tokens are never stored in browser storage, cookies, or any third-party server. All API calls run directly from your browser over HTTPS to official Google endpoints.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Habits’ use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never used for advertising, analytics profiling, data brokering, or training AI/ML models.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Data: You can export a .bin backup, switch accounts/modes, or clear browser site data at any time.
  • Google Calendar Data: You can delete the dedicated Habits Reminders calendar directly inside the app’s Reminders settings or from Google Calendar.
  • Revoking Access: You can revoke Habits’ access to your Google account at any time from Google Account Permissions .

7. Changes & Contact

If this policy is updated, the revised version and effective date will be published on this page. For privacy questions or support, please use the support contact listed on the application’s repository or Google OAuth consent screen.
